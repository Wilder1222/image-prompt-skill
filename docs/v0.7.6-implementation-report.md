# image-prompt-skill v0.7.6 Implementation Report

## Theme

**Human Presence v3 — Optical Coherence & Photographic Presence**

## Why this version exists

v0.7.5 A/B/C testing validated P9 fashion anatomy and stage locking. Test 2 established the preferred P9 visual ratio, and Test 3 preserved that proportion while modestly improving human presence. The remaining failure was concentrated: the face still read as a high-quality CG / game-rendered human.

The bottleneck was therefore no longer body proportion or prompt completeness. It was **human optical coherence**.

## Research-backed corrections

### Young skin should not become noisier just to look real

Published face-perception research associates relatively homogeneous skin coloration with youthful / healthy appearance. v0.7.6 therefore separates **skin color homogeneity** from **regional optical response**. Young skin can stay even in color while forehead, nose, cheek, eye area and lips respond differently to light.

### Eye and skin realism should agree

Research on CG faces reports that mismatched eye and skin realism can increase uncanny perception. v0.7.6 adds a Realism Coherence Guard across eyes, skin, lips, brows/lashes, hair, makeup, adjacent costume materials and camera response.

### Sclera should be natural, not over-whitened

Research on scleral brightness supports natural white sclera as a youth/health cue, but whitening beyond original values did not necessarily improve attractiveness. The eye module therefore targets natural off-white sclera, not luminous white.

### Forced asymmetry is not a universal realism requirement

Face-perception literature often reports preferences for symmetry. v0.7.6 corrects the earlier micro-asymmetry heuristic: observed natural variation may be preserved, but deliberate deformation is no longer required.

## Provider compilation

- OpenAI: one narrow edit at a time, restate locks, use accepted output as direct edit target.
- Gemini: multi-turn edit with explicit preservation plus photographic language.
- FLUX.2: identity first, positive target-state language, camera/lens cues after the subject, no negative-prompt syntax.
- Seedream: compress the internal plan into a short high-density prompt because excessively long prompts can diffuse attention.
- Midjourney Edit: prefer localized face-region edits when the rest of the asset already passes.

## Regression behavior

P9 remains higher priority than Human Presence v3. If `head_visual_too_large` and `eye_sclera_too_white` occur together, the director repairs P9 first. Once P9 passes, H2.5 locks proportion and edits only optical human-presence channels.

## Validation

All previous tests are retained with intentional routing updates. New v0.7.6 tests cover H2.5, natural sclera, youthful skin homogeneity, coherence, focus hierarchy, revised asymmetry policy and staged P9 → H2.5 routing.

The test suite verifies project logic and routing. It does not prove that any vendor model will produce a photoreal face on every run. New image A/B validation is still required.

## Prompt skeleton

Image A is the Style Restoration Reference. Image B is the Asset Master.

Keep from B: the same character identity, full-body proportion, black long hair, gold crown, black/deep-red/antique-gold costume family, shoulder armor, waist armor, layered skirt, hanging ornaments, leg accessories and complete silhouette.

Use A only for: epic mood, painterly-cinematic atmosphere, sweeping motion, luminous backlight, grand visual energy, black-white-gold-red relationship and mythic environment scale. Do not copy A's face, body or costume geometry.

Restore the image into A's visual world while applying R2 humanized realism: believable eyelids and soft tissue, regional skin reflectance, natural makeup, strand-based hair, distinct brocade/gauze/leather/metal behavior, realistic attachment points and physically coherent drape.

Lighting: cinematic directional key, weaker fill, luminous controlled rim/backlight, moderate atmospheric glow and material-specific highlights. Keep the face and costume readable; preserve painterly depth without muddy haze.

Final result: unmistakably the B character and costume design, but restored into A's epic fantasy language with convincing real-person and real-material presence.
